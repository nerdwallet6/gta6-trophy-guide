Trophy checklist

    Roadmap to Platinum

    Seven stages, in the order this guide recommends. The counts add up to all 55 trophies, and every trophy sits in exactly one stage. Roughly 200 hours in total (PowerPyx and GTABase): 60–80 hours of story, about 100 hours of checklist, about 10 hours of cleanup. One playthrough is enough, and everything is single-player.

    Missables & Saves

    Time-limited — start early

    These six are not missable, but TechWiser reports their stranger chains have windows tied to story progress. PowerPyx, on the other hand, says every trophy quest-giver is still there after the story in free roam. Only TechWiser gives the warning, so treat it as a reason to start early — not as a confirmed lockout.

      Save strategy

      1. Manual save at the start of every chapter. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so a chapter save is the only way back.
      2. Keep one untouched save before the final mission. It protects the whole run if anything goes wrong late.
      3. Keep your Chapter 1 save until Positive Mental Attitude pops. The balcony meditation is the one thing that can disappear.
      4. Never use cheats on your trophy save. GTABase and TechWiser both say cheats switch off trophies and saving; the only fix is a clean save.
      5. Watch the Irredeemable criminal profile. PowerPyx and GTABase both say it is permanent — check what it means before going on a rampage.

      Sources & honesty

      Every trophy in this guide cites the sources its numbers and methods come from. The guide itself is written in our own words — the sources are the evidence, not the text.

      • PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap. The main roadmap source: story order, missable warning, 200-hour estimate, 42 shooting-range golds, 14 Top Fun locations, 40 race locations, 9 activity types.
      • GTABase — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Achievements List, and the 100% Completion Guide. The numbers source: 250 of 397 stunts (130 jumps, 202 bridges, 40 knife flights, 25 base jumps), 39 gang operations, 9 gangs, 8 garages at ~$160,000, the 45/45/10 checklist split.
      • EGamers.io — GTA 6 Trophy List, and the 100% Completion checklist guide. The cross-check source: trophy-vs-checklist table (stunts 250/300, races 20/30, challenges 50/70, vaults 5/8, card sets 3/15), the 27 visible / 28 hidden split.
      • TechWiser — GTA 6 Missions. The stranger-chain source: chapter-by-chapter mission lists, character requirements, and the single-source warning that stranger chains have windows tied to story progress.
      • KeenGamer — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Platinum Roadmap. The second opinion on the roadmap, the missable warning, and the 42 shooting-range golds.
      • Output Lag — GTA 6 Trophy List. The visible-list cross-check: all 27 non-hidden trophies with grades, and the 200-of-277 vehicle figure for 100%.
      • Exophase (via Push Square) — the PS5 trophy list the 28-hidden count comes from: 27 visible, 28 hidden, 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze.

      What is confirmed, and what is not

      • Confirmed by two or more sources: the 55 names, descriptions and grades; the 27 visible / 28 hidden split; the stunt breakdown (250 of 397); the 18 meditation spots; the 42 shooting-range golds; the 14 Top Fun locations; the trophy-vs-checklist numbers.
      • Confirmed by one source only: the TechWiser warning about stranger-chain windows; the GTABase figures for Actions Speak Louder (500 profile actions) and Personal Best (max Strength for both characters); the stranger-count list for Strangers & Freaks. Each is labelled where it appears.
      • Unconfirmed at launch: In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin — PowerPyx marks all five as not yet known — plus which animal Fair Game wants. Their guides say so plainly instead of guessing.
      • Still open: whether the Chapter 1 balcony meditation is actually required for Positive Mental Attitude. PowerPyx and GTABase both say it is unconfirmed. The safe play is to do it — PowerPyx's author did it and then earned the trophy.
