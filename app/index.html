Seven stages, 55 trophies, each in exactly one stage. Finish the story, start the strangers early, then grind the long tail — A Long Road is the capstone, not the start.

Only one trophy can be missed. Six more are time-limited. Everything else can be finished in free roam after the credits — but only if your saves are right.

Save strategy

  1. Manual save at the start of every chapter. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so a chapter-start save is your only way back.
  2. Keep one save from before the final mission. You cannot replay the finale.
  3. Do Lucia's balcony meditation in Chapter 1 (the morning after Down to Earth) before you finish the chapter — see Positive Mental Attitude.
  4. Do not use cheats. Turning on a cheat switches off trophies, and you cannot save anymore once you have used one. All 61 cheats unlock only after the story anyway.
  5. Nothing on the 100% checklist is missable. GTABase and PowerPyx both say every checklist requirement can still be done in free roam after the credits.

The stranger-window warning is single-source: TechWiser says quest-givers may stop offering missions, while PowerPyx says quest-givers return after the story. Starting the strands early costs nothing and covers both readings.

Every guide names its sources. Where the guides disagree or do not know yet, this page says so instead of guessing.

    Honesty notes

    • Confirmed: the 55 names, descriptions and grades match across PowerPyx, GTABase, EGamers.io, Push Square and Exophase. The 27 visible / 28 hidden split is the same everywhere.
    • Single-source: the stranger time-limited warning comes from TechWiser alone. PowerPyx says quest-givers return after the story. This guide flags all six as time-limited and tells you why.
    • Unconfirmed: In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin have no confirmed unlock method yet (PowerPyx marks them not yet known), and Fair Game's animal is not pinned down. Their guides say so plainly.
    • Balcony nuance: the Chapter 1 balcony meditation adds +1 to the Positive Mental Attitude counter but is not marked as a spot. PowerPyx says it is unconfirmed whether it is required; its author did it and earned the trophy, so this guide tells you to do it.
    • Counts that differ by source: challenges are 50 of 80 for the trophy (the 100% checklist wants 70 of 80); races are 20 of 40 (100% wants 30 of 40); card sets are 3 of 15 for the trophy (18 sets exist, 100% wants 15).
    • Checked 18–19 Nov 2026. The game launched 19 Nov 2026 and launch-era guides are still being corrected. Re-check the Sources tab before you hunt anything marked unconfirmed.
