⚠ Only one trophy can be missed

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida — is the only missable trophy on the list. Everything else can be earned after the story.

The risk sits in chapter 1: after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia can be found in the kitchen at Jason's house in the morning. Follow her to the balcony and choose Accept to meditate there. That balcony spot is not marked as a Meditation Spot, and whether it is required is still unconfirmed — the PowerPyx author earned the trophy after doing it. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so make a manual save at the start of every chapter.

Roadmap to the platinum

Difficulty 4/10, about 200 hours, one playthrough. Cheats disable trophies and saving — play clean.

  1. Stage 1 — Story

    Play chapters 1–4 and the final mission. Banks the 12 story trophies, including Gone Fishing, the four chapter trophies, the six named missions and the gold finale. Make a manual save at the start of every chapter.

    12 trophies

  2. Stage 2 — Side strands

    Finish the five side characters' final missions (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone) and 8 stranger strands. Watch for Yago's chapter 3 race — its hourglass icon means it is time-sensitive.

    6 trophies

  3. Stage 3 — Activities

    Stunts, races, the Shooting Range, drone races, fishing, bowling, heists and the one-off trophies. The 100% groundwork happens here: 250 stunts, 42 gold range medals, 20 race wins, 50 challenges, 500 profile actions.

    30 trophies

  4. Stage 4 — Collectibles

    Sweep Leonida once, properly: 3 card sets, WAiNK at 100 locations, 100 vehicles discovered, all 52 areas explored, all 8 garages bought, 10 vehicles registered to Scooter Bros.

    6 trophies

  5. Stage 5 — Cleanup & 100%

    Open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion (it unlocks after the story), clear whatever it still lists, double-check the meditation counter, and the platinum pops.

    1 trophy + platinum
