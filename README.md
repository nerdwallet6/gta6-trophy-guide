Leonida Nights: the complete GTA VI trophy guide — all 55 trophies with a full guide each, a 7-stage roadmap to Platinum, missable and time-limited warnings, and an offline progress tracker that saves on your device.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

## What is inside

- **Tracker (first screen):** compact header, progress card with a live Next Up, and a two-column tracker — checklist on the left, the selected trophy's full guide on the right (steps, tip, sources, missable / time-limited warnings). Search, grade and kind filters, an earned-only filter, a spoiler toggle (hidden trophies stay secret until earned), and export / import / reset of progress via localStorage.
- **Roadmap to Platinum:** 7 stages, each trophy in exactly one stage — Story 12, Side stories 6, Races, stunts & games 10, Combat, heists & robberies 10, Exploration & the long tail 10, Collectibles & hunts 6, Platinum 1. Each stage card has a progress bar and a clickable trophy list.
- **Missables & Saves:** the only missable trophy (Positive Mental Attitude, with its Chapter 1 balcony warning), the 6 time-limited stranger trophies, and the save strategy — manual save at the start of every chapter, no mission replay, no New Game+, cheats switch off trophies and saving.
- **Sources & honesty:** every trophy cites 3–4 sources (PowerPyx, GTABase, EGamers.io, TechWiser, KeenGamer, Output Lag, Push Square). Unconfirmed trophies say so plainly instead of guessing.
- **Fully offline:** the page makes no network requests except loading its own `app/data/trophies.json`. No trackers, no fonts, no images from the network.

## The numbers

55 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze; 27 visible, 28 hidden. Kinds: Story 12, Side story 7, Collectibles 3, Activities 12, Robbery 6, Exploration & misc 13, 100% completion 2. Exact figures with sources: Cunning Stunts 250 of 397 (130 stunt jumps, 202 under-the-bridge, 40 knife flights, 25 base jumps; 100% wants 300); The Shootist 42 golds across 12 ranges; Top Fun 14 locations / 42 races; races 20 of 40 (100% wants 30); challenges 50 of 80 (100% wants 70); vaults 5 of 8; card sets 3 of 15; garages 8 at about $160,000; 9 gangs / 39 operations; Positive Mental Attitude 18 spots at 10 locations.

## Data freshness

Names and descriptions are the official English text as shown on PlayStation Network, cross-checked across PowerPyx, GTABase, EGamers.io, Push Square and Exophase on 18–19 Nov 2026. The game launched 19 Nov 2026 and launch-era guides are still being corrected: In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin have no confirmed unlock method yet, and Fair Game's animal is not pinned down. The stranger-window warning is single-source (TechWiser); PowerPyx says quest-givers return after the story.
